Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026

elevendash is free, open-source software that you run on your own computer. There is no elevendash server, account or analytics. The people who make elevendash never receive your logs, your match data or anything about you.

Your Eleven logs

Eleven Table Tennis writes log files on your headset. Besides your matches they contain your Eleven and Meta account tokens, your platform user id and name, the names of people you played online and sometimes your location. elevendash:

  • copies the logs from your Quest to a folder on your computer that you choose (your workspace), using two read-only requests to Eleven's File Sharing on your own network: /getListing and /downloadFile. It never uploads anything to the headset and never deletes anything there;
  • keeps the logs only in your workspace and never sends them anywhere;
  • writes a dashboard that holds match data only (scores, points, shots, dates), never log lines, tokens, ids or account names. Who you share the dashboard with is up to you.

elevendash redact makes a copy of a log without tokens, ids, names, network addresses and location, for the rare case you need to show one. Read the copy before you share it.

Optional: YouTube uploads

If you choose to set it up, the youtube/ part of elevendash uploads a session's headset recording and a highlight reel to your own YouTube channel. It uses Google's official API libraries and an OAuth client that you create in your own Google Cloud project. It asks for two permissions:

  • youtube.upload, to upload the videos you choose, always as unlisted;
  • youtube.readonly, to show which channel you signed in to and to check that each upload has finished processing and is unlisted.

It cannot edit or delete anything on your channel. The sign-in token is saved on your computer in ~/.eleven-youtube and is sent only to Google. Video ids go into videos.json in your workspace so the dashboard can link to them. elevendash does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell or transfer it, and does not use it for anything other than these uploads.

elevendash's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploaded videos are subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove elevendash's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the token by deleting ~/.eleven-youtube.

Optional: PDF

elevendash pdf prints the dashboard with a browser already installed on your computer (Chrome, Edge or Chromium), from the dashboard file on your computer, in a temporary browser profile that is deleted afterwards.

This website

This site and the demo dashboard are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The demo is built from generated matches and contains no real player's data.

Changes and questions

Changes to this page are made in the open, in the project's repository. Questions: open an issue. For anything security-related, use private vulnerability reporting, and never attach a raw log.
